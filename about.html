About Us

Hi, I am Swastik Moharana, founder of QUICO, a technology and cybersecurity venture based in Odisha, India. I build practical tools and training that make privacy and cryptography easier to understand.

Why this site exists

Most online security tools ask you to upload files or type secrets into a server you cannot see. The Crypto & Security Utility Suite takes the opposite approach: everything runs locally in your browser, so your images, messages and passwords never leave your device.

What you will find here

  • Image Steganography Studio for hiding and revealing text in PNG images
  • Hash generator (SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-384, SHA-512)
  • Password strength auditor with entropy and crack-time estimates

Our focus

Privacy, applied cryptography and cybersecurity awareness. The code is open source under the MIT License.
